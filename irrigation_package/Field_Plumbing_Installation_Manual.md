## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & HYDRAULIC DESIGN SUMMARY

2. **Zone 2: Plot A (East Field) — PKM-1 Annual Drumstick**
   - **Net Planting Area:** 1.0 Acre (390 ft East-West x 130 ft North-South)
   - **Crop Spacing:** High-density 3 ft plant-to-plant along trench x 5 ft between trenches
   - **Total Population:** 3,000 Moringa trees (leaf meal & pod production)
   - **Riser Configuration:** 4 Overhead Double-Tee Stations (A1–A4 spaced at 30 ft intervals)
   - **Subline:** 1.5" (50mm OD) Class 2 PVC (120 ft total run, 8 arms of 15 ft each)
   - **Lateral Specification:** `16x4x40 Inline Pipe` (16mm LLDPE with integrated 4 LPH labyrinth drippers spaced every 40cm); 24 trenches x 390 ft = 2,926m installed
   - **Take-off Connection:** `50x16x2 Way Take-Off Collar` (V-Connector) feeding East & West simultaneously

3. **Zone 3: Plot B (West Field) — Bhagya (KDM-01) Drumstick**
   - **Net Planting Area:** 1.0 Acre (165 ft East-West x 260 ft North-South)
   - **Crop Spacing:** Commercial orchard 7 ft plant-to-plant x 8 ft between rows
   - **Total Population:** 750 Moringa trees (large fruit/export quality)
   - **Riser Configuration:** 4 Overhead Double-Tee Stations (B1–B4 spaced at 64 ft intervals)
   - **Subline:** 1.25" (40mm OD) Class 2 PVC (256 ft total run, 8 arms of 32 ft each)
   - **Lateral Specification:** Plain 16mm LLDPE pipe; 32 rows x 165 ft = 1,610m installed (Westward run only)
   - **Emitter Specification:** `Tunning Dripper (P)` (0–70 LPH adjustable turbulent flow) punched on-site every 7 ft (768 drippers total)

## 2. EXCAVATION & TRENCHING SPECIFICATIONS

Precise earthwork ensures pipeline longevity, prevents mechanical damage from inter-cultivation tractors/rotavators, and guarantees freeze/heat insulation.

| Pipeline Element | Nominal Size | Minimum Trench Depth | Minimum Trench Width | Bedding Material |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Zone 1 Mainline** | 2" (63mm) PVC | 2'-6" (750 mm) | 1'-6" (450 mm) | 2" sifted sand or loose soil |
| **Plot A Sublines** | 1.5" (50mm) PVC | 2'-0" (600 mm) | 1'-2" (350 mm) | Fine rock-free soil bed |
| **Plot B Sublines** | 1.25" (40mm) PVC | 2'-0" (600 mm) | 1'-2" (350 mm) | Fine rock-free soil bed |
| **Plot A Trenches** | 24 Trenches | 0'-8" to 1'-0" (250 mm) | 1'-0" (300 mm) | Organic compost & soil mix |
| **Plot B Rows** | 32 Surface Rows | Surface / 2" furrow | N/A | Surface laid along row stake |

### Trenching Rules:
1. **Bottom Grading:** Trenches must be free of sharp rocks, shale, roots, and clods larger than 10mm.
2. **Expansion Looping:** Never lay PVC pipes dead straight under tension. Snake the pipe slightly inside the trench (1 ft lateral deflection per 100 ft) to accommodate thermal contraction during winter nights.
3. **Backfill Protocol:** The first 6 inches of backfill must be hand-tamped sifted topsoil. Do NOT run heavy tractors over trenches until 24 hours after full backfilling.

## 4. 2" MAINLINE NETWORK LAYOUT & SOLVENT WELDING

### Master Alignment Coordinates:
- **Station 1 (Borewell Tee):** `(590, 20)` — South capped with 2" End Cap.
- **Run 1:** Northward run of **80'-0"** from `(590, 20)` to `(590, 100)`.
- **Station 2 (Corner Tee):** `(590, 100)` — East outlet capped with 2" End Cap.
- **Run 2:** Westward run of **90'-0"** from `(590, 100)` to `(500, 100)` (**Tee A**).
- **Run 3 (Inter-Plot Mainline):** Westward run of **215'-0"** from **Tee A** `(500, 100)` to **Tee B** `(285, 100)`.
  - Runs under the West half of Plot A (190 ft) + across the 25 ft buffer roadway.
- **Station 3 (Tee B):** `(285, 100)` — West outlet capped with 2" End Cap.

### PVC Solvent Welding Best Practices:
1. **Cutting:** Cut pipes squarely using a fine-tooth hacksaw blade or ratcheting PVC cutter. Remove all internal and external burrs with a deburring chamfer tool.
2. **Dry Fit:** Check dry fit of pipe into fitting socket; it should enter 1/3 to 2/3 of the depth snugly.
3. **Cleaning:** Wipe pipe ends and fitting interior with a dry cotton cloth.
4. **Solvent Application:** Using the provided 3L heavy-duty PVC Solvent Cement, apply a uniform, generous layer around the pipe exterior and a thin, even layer inside the socket.
5. **Assembly:** Push the pipe firmly home with a 1/4 turn twist to distribute the cement evenly. Hold rigidly for 30 seconds to prevent push-out.
6. **Cure Time:** Allow **minimum 2 hours** initial set before moving pipes, and **24 hours full cure** before pressure testing.

## 5. 4-FOOT OVERHEAD DOUBLE-TEE RISER MANIFOLD ASSEMBLY

The proprietary overhead double-tee manifold provides waist-height ergonomics (+4'-0" above finished grade), eliminating the need for operators to crawl into muddy pits to turn valves or monitor subline pressures.

### Assembly Sequence (Standard for all 8 Riser Stations):
1. **Buried Mainline Tee:** In the mainline trench bottom (-2'-6"), solvent-weld a `2" PVC Equal Tee` with the side outlet pointing vertically (+Z).
2. **Vertical Riser Pipe:** Glue a **4'-0" cut length of 2" PVC pipe** into the tee outlet, extending straight up through finished grade level to +4.0 ft.
3. **Overhead Double-Tee:** Glue a `2" PVC Equal Tee` at the top of the 4ft riser, oriented horizontally with branches facing North and South.
4. **Reducers:**
   - **Plot A Risers (A1–A4):** Glue a `2" x 1.5" (50mm) PVC Bushing Reducer` into both North and South tee outlets.
   - **Plot B Risers (B1–B4):** Glue a `2" x 1.25" (40mm) PVC Bushing Reducer` into both North and South tee outlets.
5. **Ball Valves:**
   - **Plot A:** Solvent-weld a `50mm (1.5") Plain Ball Valve` (Item #13) onto each reducer stub.
   - **Plot B:** Solvent-weld a `40mm (1.25") Plain Ball Valve` (Item #12) onto each reducer stub.
   - *Ensure valve handle operates smoothly and points upwards.*
6. **Overhead Elbow & Drop Pipe:**
   - Glue a 90° PVC Elbow onto the valve discharge pointing directly downward.
   - Solvent-weld a **6'-0" cut length** of drop pipe (1.5" for Plot A, 1.25" for Plot B) down into the subline trench (-2'-0" elevation).
7. **Trench Elbow & Subline Connection:**
   - Glue a bottom 90° PVC Elbow at the trench bottom, turning horizontally into the subline trench.
   - Connect the horizontal buried subline pipe.
8. **Subline Flush Valve:**
   - At the terminal end of every subline arm, install a `50mm Flush Valve` (Plot A) or `40mm Flush Valve` (Plot B).
   - Enclose the flush valve in an 8" PVC sleeve pipe with a removable cap flush with the ground for easy flushing access.

  - **Riser A2:** `(500, 160)` — Subline arms cover `Y = 145'` to `175'`
  - **Riser A3:** `(500, 190)` — Subline arms cover `Y = 175'` to `205'`
  - **Riser A4:** `(500, 220)` — Subline arms cover `Y = 205'` to `235'`

- **Trenches:** 24 trenches spaced exactly **5'-0" apart** (3 trenches per 15-ft arm x 8 arms = 24 trenches).

### Collar Drilling & Lateral Unrolling:
1. **Drilling:** Using a 16mm spade bit or hole saw, drill into the top center of the buried 1.5" PVC subline at exact 5-foot intervals. Clean out all plastic shavings from inside the pipe.
2. **Collar Installation:** Seat the rubber grommet firmly into the hole. Clamp the `50x16x2 Way Take of Coller` (Item #9) around the 50mm pipe, securing the barbed stem through the grommet.
3. **Lateral Laying:** Unroll the `16x4x40 Inline Pipe` (Item #8) carefully along each trench bed. Do not drag the pipe over rough gravel to avoid scratching the LLDPE wall.
4. **Length:** Cut each trench lateral to **390 ft total length** (~195 ft West and ~195 ft East of the central subline).
5. **Connection:** Push the East and West pipe ends firmly over the two barbed outlets of the 2-way collar. Secure with safety lock rings.
6. **End Capping:** At the far East (`X = 695`) and far West (`X = 305`) ends, install `16mm Endcaps` (Item #4) with figure-8 folds.

## 8. SYSTEM FLUSHING & PRESSURE TESTING PROTOCOL

Flushing removes sand, PVC burrs, cement debris, and insects before water enters sensitive dripper labyrinths. **Flushing must always proceed strictly from largest diameter to smallest diameter.**

```
[STAGE A: Mainline Flush] 
   └── Remove 2" end caps at (500, 245) & (285, 340) -> Run pump at full flow for 5 minutes.
         │
         ▼
[STAGE B: Subline Flush]
   └── Close mainline caps; open all 16 Flush Valves (50mm & 40mm) -> Flush until water runs clear.
         │
         ▼
[STAGE C: Lateral Flush]
   └── Close flush valves; remove 16mm end caps from laterals -> Flush for 3 minutes per zone.
         │
         ▼
[STAGE D: Pressure & Leak Test]
   └── Cap all ends; pressurize system to 2.5 kg/cm² for 60 minutes -> Inspect all 120+ joints.
```

### Pressure Test Acceptance Criteria:
1. Zero weeping or dripping at all solvent-welded joints, ball valve unions, and filter flanges.
2. Pressure loss after pump shutoff must not exceed **0.2 kg/cm² in 15 minutes**.
3. All 3,000 inline drippers in Plot A and 768 online drippers in Plot B must show uniform wetting patterns with no dry skips or blowing emitters.

## 9. OPERATION & PREVENTATIVE MAINTENANCE

### Recommended Daily Irrigation Schedule:
- **Summer (Peak Vegetative & Pod Stage):** 35–45 Liters per plant daily.
  - Plot A (PKM-1): Run 2 Riser Stations simultaneously for 45 minutes; repeat for remaining 2 stations.
  - Plot B (Bhagya): Run 2 Riser Stations simultaneously for 40 minutes (drippers tuned to 35 LPH).
- **Winter / Rainy Season:** 15–20 Liters per plant every 2–3 days.

### Routine Maintenance Schedule:
- **Daily:** Open filter drain ball valve for 15 seconds while pump runs to purge trapped silt.
- **Weekly:** Unscrew filter housing, remove disc cartridge, and wash under high-pressure hose.
- **Monthly:** Open all 16 subline flush valves and 80 lateral endcaps for 2 minutes to flush accumulated silt.
- **Seasonal (Every 6 Months):** Acid treatment using 0.5% commercial Hydrochloric Acid (HCl) to dissolve calcium carbonate scaling and fertilizer salts in emitter labyrinths.
