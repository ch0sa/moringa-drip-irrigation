## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This package provides a comprehensive, precision-engineered **Classic Architectural Blueprint and Field Execution Package** for a 2-Acre Moringa orchard in a semi-arid zone powered by a **5HP Solar Borewell**. 

The design resolves all field hydraulic, zoning, and ergonomic requirements:
1. **Plot A (PKM-1):** High-density leaf/drumstick crop (1 Acre, 3,000 plants on a 3×5 ft grid) arranged in 24 double-wing trenches served by 4 dedicated riser stations (`A1`–`A4` @ 30 ft intervals) with 50mm ball valves and 1.5" PVC sublines.
2. **Plot B (Bhagya):** Commercial perennial drumstick orchard (1 Acre, 750 plants on a 7×8 ft grid) arranged in 32 single-wing westward rows served by 4 dedicated riser stations (`B1`–`B4` @ 54–64 ft intervals) with 40mm ball valves and 1.25" PVC sublines.
3. **Overhead Double-Tee Manifolds:** Eliminates buried valve boxes by bringing all valve controls to **+4'-0" waist height** above finished grade for ergonomic, tool-free zone switching and maintenance.

All technical drawings strictly adhere to a **Classic Blueprint Prussian Blue (`#0B2545`) and Pure White** visual design system, with zero text overlapping, verified mathematical coordinates, and multi-format CAD/vector/PDF exports.

## 2. Core Field Geometry & Hydraulic Parameters

### A. Water Source & Mainline Distribution
* **Source:** 5HP Solar Borewell Head located at `(X = 3060, Y = 2500)` in drawing coordinates / field coordinate equivalent `(590 ft, 20 ft)`.
* **Head Assembly:** Solar Borewell Submersible → 2" NRV (Non-Return Valve) → 2" Brass/Bronze Gate Valve → 2" 120-Mesh 'T'-Type Disc/Screen Filter → 2" Corner Tee (South stub capped with 2" End Cap).
* **Mainline Segment 1 (North Run):** 80'-0" of 2" (63mm OD) Class 2 PVC pipe running North to Corner Tee `(3060, 2100)`.
* **Mainline Segment 2 (East-West Run to Tee A):** 90'-0" of 2" PVC running West from Corner Tee to **Tee A** `(2610, 2100)`.
* **Mainline Segment 3 (Inter-Plot Corridor Run):** 215'-0" of 2" PVC running West under Plot A's centerline to **Tee B** `(1185, 2100)`.
* **Open Annotation Corridor:** A dedicated 90 ft (450 px) open buffer separates the West edge of Plot A (`X = 1635`) from Plot B (`X = 1185`) to guarantee complete visual isolation and prevent line clutter.

### B. Plot A Specifications (PKM-1 Variety)
* **Net Area:** 1.0 Acre (390 ft East-West × 120 ft North-South active bed zone).
* **Spacing & Density:** 3 ft plant-to-plant × 5 ft trench-to-trench = 3,000 total plants.
* **Layout:** 24 horizontal trenches (12 trenches North of Tee A, 12 trenches South).
* **2" Center Mainline:** Runs North 145'-0" from Tee A along the central spine, terminating at `Y = 1375` with a **2" End Cap**.
* **Riser Stations:** 4 stations spaced at 30'-0" centers:
  * `A1 (30')` — Elev `Y = 1950`
  * `A2 (60')` — Elev `Y = 1800`
  * `A3 (90')` — Elev `Y = 1650`
  * `A4 (120')` — Elev `Y = 1500`
* **Station Plumbing:** Each station consists of a 4 ft vertical 2" riser, an overhead 2" double-tee, two 2"×1.5" reducers, two 50mm ball valves, 6 ft drop pipes, and buried 1.5" (50mm) sublines feeding East and West wings.
* **Drip Lateral:** 16mm inline drip tubing with 40cm emitter spacing (2.0 LPH per dripper), 2 lateral lines per trench bed.
* **Flush Valves:** 50mm (1.5") ball/flush valves installed at both North (`Y = 1425`) and South (`Y = 2025`) subline terminals in 8" access sleeves.

### C. Plot B Specifications (Bhagya Variety)
* **Net Area:** 1.0 Acre (165 ft East-West × 256 ft North-South active orchard zone).
* **Spacing & Density:** 7 ft plant-to-plant × 8 ft row-to-row = 750 total trees.
* **Layout:** 32 horizontal rows projecting **Westward only** from the subline.
* **Mainline Run & Offset:**
  * **24 ft Bare Mainline:** 2" PVC runs 24'-0" North from Tee B `(1185, 2100)` to `Y = 1980` through a clear equipment accessway (no rows/trees).
  * **216 ft Active Mainline:** 2" PVC continues 216'-0" further North from `Y = 1980` to `Y = 900` (`Riser B4`).
  * **Total 2" Mainline:** **240'-0" total** 2" pipe North of Tee B.
  * **Mainline Termination:** Terminates directly at **Riser B4** `(Y = 900)` with a **2" End Cap**.
* **1.25" Subline Extension:** North of Riser B4, **only the 1.25" subline continues** 32'-0" North (from `Y = 900` to `Y = 740`), terminating at the top 40mm Flush Valve.
* **Riser Stations:** 4 stations:
  * `B1` — Elev `Y = 1710`
  * `B2` — Elev `Y = 1440`
  * `B3` — Elev `Y = 1170`
  * `B4` — Elev `Y = 900` (Mainline terminus)
* **Station Plumbing:** Each station features a 4 ft vertical 2" riser, overhead 2" double-tee, two 2"×1.25" reducers, two 40mm ball valves, 6 ft drop pipes, and buried 1.25" (40mm) sublines.
* **Drip Lateral:** Plain 16mm LDPE tubing with online button drippers (4.0 LPH) punched every 7'-0" directly at the tree base.
* **Flush Valves:** 40mm (1.25") flush valves installed at both North (`Y = 740`) and South (`Y = 1980`) subline ends.

## 6. Field Execution & Installation Guide

### Phase 1: Trenching & Mainline Burial
1. Excavate the 2" mainline trench to a depth of **2 ft 6 in (750 mm)** and width of 1 ft 6 in (450 mm).
2. Lay 2" (63mm) Class 2 PVC pipe starting from the Solar Borewell head `(590, 20)` North to Corner Tee `(590, 100)`.
3. Turn West 90 ft to install **Tee A** `(500, 100)`.
4. Continue West 215 ft to install **Tee B** `(285, 100)`. Cap the South stub of the Borewell Tee, East stub of Corner Tee, and West stub of Tee B with 2" PVC End Caps solvent-welded with heavy-duty PVC cement.
5. In Plot B, run 24 ft of bare 2" pipe North before installing the first riser feed. Continue 216 ft North to Riser B4 and cap the 2" mainline with a 2" End Cap.
6. Bed all buried pipes with 3 in (75 mm) of stone-free topsoil or sifted sand before backfilling.

### Phase 2: Overhead Riser Standpipe Assembly
1. At each riser location (A1–A4, B1–B4), solvent-weld a 2" equal tee facing upward.
2. Cut a 2" vertical standpipe to extend **4'-0" (1.22 m) above finished grade**. Anchor the base with an earth stake or concrete footing sleeve.
3. Glue a 2" overhead double-tee at waist level.
4. Solvent-weld bushing reducers:
   * **Plot A:** 2" × 1.5" (63×50mm) Bushing Reducers.
   * **Plot B:** 2" × 1.25" (63×40mm) Bushing Reducers.
5. Install high-grade PVC plain ball valves (50mm for Plot A, 40mm for Plot B).
6. Connect 90° overhead elbows and 6 ft drop pipes descending vertically into the subline trench.

### Phase 3: Subline Trenching & Lateral Take-Offs
1. Excavate subline trenches to a depth of **2 ft 0 in (600 mm)**.
2. In Plot A, run 1.5" (50mm) PVC sublines North and South. At each of the 24 trench locations, install a **50×16×2-way takeoff collar (V-connector)** with rubber grommet to feed dual 16mm inline laterals East and West.
3. In Plot B, run 1.25" (40mm) PVC sublines. At each of the 32 row locations, install a **40×16×1-way takeoff collar** to feed a single 16mm plain lateral Westward.
4. Install terminal flush valves (50mm in Plot A, 40mm in Plot B) housed inside 8" PVC surface inspection sleeves with slip caps.

### Phase 4: Flushing & Commissioning
1. **Initial High-Velocity Flush:** Before attaching end caps or laterals, open all subline flush valves and operate the 5HP solar pump for 10 minutes to clear all dirt, PVC shavings, and grit.
2. **Zone Leak & Pressure Test:** Close all flush valves and pressurize the system to 2.5 kg/cm². Inspect all solvent-welded joints, ball valves, and drop elbows for pressure drops or seepage.
3. **Lateral Connection & Emitter Verification:** Connect 16mm lateral pipes. Run water through each line before sealing lateral end plugs. In Plot B, verify that all button drippers discharge 4.0 LPH at 1.5–2.0 bar operating pressure.

## 7. Operational Schedule & Preventive Maintenance

### Daily Irrigation Rotation (Solar Window: 9:00 AM – 3:30 PM)
* **Cycle 1 (Plot A — PKM-1 High Density):** Open Valves A1 & A2 (9:00 AM – 11:00 AM). Open Valves A3 & A4 (11:00 AM – 1:00 PM).
* **Cycle 2 (Plot B — Bhagya Drumstick Orchard):** Open Valves B1 & B2 (1:00 PM – 2:15 PM). Open Valves B3 & B4 (2:15 PM – 3:30 PM).

### Maintenance Cadence
* **Daily:** Check solar inverter frequency, pump operating pressure at head gauge (target: 2.5–3.0 kg/cm²), and inspect filter pressure differential.
* **Weekly:** Open terminal flush valves (50mm on Plot A, 40mm on Plot B) for 90 seconds while running the pump to evacuate silt accumulation.
* **Monthly:** Disassemble the 2" T-strainer disc filter, wash disc cartridges in a dilute acidic solution (pH 4.0) or water bath, and inspect all riser ball valve handles.
* **Quarterly:** Walk lateral lines to inspect button drippers for mineral or algae clogging. Replace damaged grommets using spare 16mm take-off sets.
